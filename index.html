What the Foundational Diagnostic Examines

Foundational Diagnostic Report

A Python-based diagnostic tool designed to provide a clear, plain-language overview of time-series data before deeper analysis or forecasting.

Example Dataset

Daily average temperature observations from Doerun, Georgia are used here to demonstrate the analysis on a naturally variable and seasonal time series.

What It Examines

The report evaluates summary statistics, outliers, trend behavior, distributional structure, autocorrelation, periodicity, and changes in local variability.

Example Findings

The example demonstrates strong short-term persistence, seasonal structure, changing local variability, and the importance of interpreting spectral results carefully.

Time-Series Stability Analysis

A deeper time-series analysis designed to examine whether the behavior of a dataset remains consistent over time or changes across different periods.

Purpose

The Stability Analysis uses resampling methods to examine how consistently statistical characteristics of a dataset persist across repeated samples. Rather than simply describing the observed time series, it evaluates how sensitive analytical results are to changes in the sampled data.

What It Examines

The analysis investigates changes in variability, distributional behavior, trend structure, periodic behavior, and other characteristics that may indicate shifts in the underlying behavior of the series.

Relationship to the Foundational Diagnostic

This analysis is intended as a deeper second-stage examination for datasets where the initial diagnostic suggests that behavior may differ across time.

What This Analysis Examines
